What we do — and why children come first

Evidence-based tools
built around what's best for children.

Custody decisions shape a child's daily life for years. They deserve to be made with clear evidence, consistent standards, and a genuine understanding of what that child's life actually looks like. We are currently focused on Georgia family courts — working within Georgia law and the communities of Cobb, Fulton, and Gwinnett counties — and we use what we learn to advocate for the safeguards, standards, and accountability that custody decisions involving children should always have.

1

Evidence organization

We take your documents, messages, transcripts, and records and transform them into a structured, searchable evidence record — organized around your child's daily life, wellbeing, and the behavioral patterns that affect them.

2

Pattern analysis

Using a validated analytical framework, we analyze your evidence to surface patterns if they exist — behaviors that affect your child's access to both parents, stability, care, and wellbeing. We document what the evidence shows, accurately and completely. We do not build narratives the evidence does not support.

3

Reform through data

With your consent, your anonymized case contributes to our research on how Georgia family courts make decisions affecting children — helping us build the evidentiary foundation for enforceable standards, professional accountability, and safeguards that put children's wellbeing at the center of every custody decision.

4

Support and guidance

Our support tier includes one-on-one consultations to help you understand your evidence record, navigate the tool, and communicate your child's reality more effectively. Written guides are available for self-directed use over time. This is not legal advice — it is informed support for understanding and presenting what your documentation shows about your child's life.
